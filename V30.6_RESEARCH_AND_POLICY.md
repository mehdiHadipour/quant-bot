# V30.6 Session & Symbol Policy

## Session weighting
- London/NY overlap: weight 1.00, threshold 65
- Asia/Europe overlap: weight 0.86, threshold 68
- London: weight 0.88, threshold 65
- New York: weight 0.92, threshold 72
- Asia: weight 0.72, threshold 76

Rationale: major session overlaps generally have higher liquidity/volatility; Tokyo-London is historically less active than London-New York. The policy therefore uses weights and thresholds rather than treating all sessions equally.
