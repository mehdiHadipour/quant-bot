ROOT CAUSE
The 96/1 SELL/BUY imbalance in V30.2 is primarily caused by the hard requirement that 4H and 1D EMA(50/200) biases agree, combined with a strongly bearish 1D regime in the supplied period. Raw sweep/MSS/FVG primitives are broadly symmetric. Removing/softening the daily gate increased trade count and BUYs but worsened net R on this dataset, so those variants were rejected rather than being called an improvement.
